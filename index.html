Topology

VLAN 20 · 1 GbE switched fabric

Switch

  • TP-Link TL-SG108 — 8 × 1 GbE unmanaged
  • Management address 10.10.20.2
  • Two ports left spare for the guest VLAN

Power

  • APC BX950MI — 640 VA, line interactive
  • Outlets reserved for the 3090 SFF and the M910q
  • 7050 Micro runs on the switched outlet

Traffic

  • Cluster heartbeat and migration over the primary link
  • Replication and backups between node 01 and node 03
  • Storage throughput caps at roughly 110 MB/s per link

About

ProxVM · Bamoyski Studios

Everything here runs on hardware I own, documented properly.

These three machines came out of a business refresh cycle. They were cheap, quiet, and identical enough that a single binder covers the lot. ProxVM manages them; this page is the part the software cannot tell you.

The specifications below reflect what is actually installed, not the maximum the platform allows. Where a number depends on the configuration, the notes say so.

ProxVM is the open-source control plane that sits on top of this hardware. Bamoyski Studios runs game servers and homelab VMs on the same nodes.
