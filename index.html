001 / flagship

ProxVM

VM management for Proxmox VE & Apache Guacamole.

ProxVM turns a homelab into a real control plane: cloud-init provisioning, a credential vault, role-based access, and RDP/SSH/VNC sessions — all against actual Proxmox and Guacamole. No mock data, no simulated infrastructure.

02 / About

The person behind the uptime.

A short version of how I got here, and what I keep chasing.

I like the part where it becomes real.

Most of what I know came from breaking things in my own lab and rebuilding them until they held. That's still the loop: read the docs, trace the failure, ship the fix, write it down.

Today I'm building ProxVM, an open-source control plane for Proxmox VE and Apache Guacamole, and running Bamoyski Studios, where I host game servers and homelab VMs on hardware I own. No oversold slices, no ticket queue between you and the person who runs the machines.

~/homelab

Proxmox VE, Cloudflare in front, PostgreSQL and Redis behind it, and a lab that exists to be broken and rebuilt.

What I optimize for
  1. 01

    Truth over polish

    Real errors beat fake loading states. If something is down, say so and show why.

  2. 02

    Own the stack

    Self-hosted where it makes sense. Know where the data lives and who can touch it.

  3. 03

    Make it legible

    Infrastructure and interfaces should explain themselves without a manual.
